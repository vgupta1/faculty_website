Research Active Faculty in Data Sciences and Operations

Data Sciences and Operations consists of three groups: Operations Management, Statistics, and Information Systems. For PhD candidates in either the Operations Management or Statistics program, you must select a primary advisor from among the research active (tenure-track or tenured) faculty in that group. You are welcome to have a co-advisor from any group (or even outside the department or school!), provided your primary advisor agrees.

Note that at present, we do not have a PhD Program in Information Systems, and, hence IS faculty cannot serve as your primary advisor.

To assist you, here is a list of research active faculty by group and a link to their webpage where you can learn more about them and their research interests.
